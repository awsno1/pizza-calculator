Ingredient Sub-Recipes

🔴 Red Base Sauces

Classic Tomato Sauce
Ingredients: Mutti Polpa canned tomatoes, extra virgin olive oil (EVOO), Diamond Crystal Kosher salt (1g salt per 100g tomatoes).
  1. Partially drain the tomatoes to remove excess watery juice.
  2. Add a small glug of high-quality EVOO.
  3. Mix in 1g of salt per 100g of tomatoes until well combined.
Vodka Sauce
Ingredients: 200g Mutti Polpa canned tomatoes, 80ml cooking cream, 1 garlic clove (thinly sliced), 1 shallot (diced), 20ml vodka, parmesan.
  1. Sauté shallot with half a teaspoon of Kosher salt in olive oil over low heat until fragrant. Add garlic and dried chilli flakes (optional) when shallots are caramelised.
  2. Stir in tomato paste and cook down until darkened. Add canned tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes.
  3. Add cream and stir. Blitz with immersion blender. Add vodka and cook for 1-2 minutes. Add parmesan.

🟢 Green Base Sauces

Green Sauce
Ingredients: 30g basil leaves, 20g rocket, ½ garlic clove (chopped), 30ml EVOO, 5ml lemon juice, salt, pepper, 1–2 tbsp warm water (as needed).
  1. Blanch basil leaves in boiling water for 15 seconds.
  2. Remove immediately to an ice bath and drain thoroughly once cooled. Pat dry with paper towels.
  3. Blitz basil, rocket, garlic, EVOO, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a blender.
  4. Mix in warm water (1 teaspoon at a time) to thin out the sauce to desired consistency.

🟡 Cooked Toppings & Caramelisation

Caramelised Onions
Ingredients: Brown onions, cooking oil, Kosher salt, water.
  1. Finely slice the brown onions against the grain.
  2. Add onions to a wide pan on low heat with a splash of oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Cook slowly on very low heat for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep golden brown.
  4. Add a small splash of water if the pan dries out before the onions fully soften.
Caramelised Pineapple
Ingredients: Fresh pineapple, extra virgin olive oil (EVOO), brown sugar.
  1. Slice fresh pineapple into thin strips (approx. 5mm wide) against the grain.
  2. Preheat a skillet over medium heat with a light coating of EVOO.
  3. Pan-fry pineapple slices until lightly browned, then flip.
  4. Sprinkle brown sugar over the top to coat evenly once melted.
  5. Cook until sugar caramelises into a rich glaze, remove from heat, and cool before topping.

⚪ Creams & Finishers

Lemon Stracciatella
Ingredients: Fresh stracciatella (or shredded burrata), fresh lemon zest, flaky sea salt, EVOO.
  1. Place fresh stracciatella in a small bowl.
  2. Gently fold in fresh lemon zest and a tiny drizzle of EVOO.
  3. Finish with flaky sea salt and dollop post-bake over hot pizza.
