Ingredients Needed

  • Bread Flour (30%) 290.7 g

  • Main Water (95%) 561.5 g

  • Olive Oil 19.0 g
  • Sugar 15.0 g
  • Instant Yeast 1.55 g

Method & Mixing Instructions

  1. Yeast Bloom & Water Prep: Pour cold main water (561.5g) into your mixer bowl, setting aside your reserved water (29.5g) in a small cup. Add instant yeast (1.55g) and swirl around until fully dissolved.
  2. Autolyse / Initial Mix: Let sit for 1 minute. Add Tipo 00 flour (678.3g) and Bread flour (290.7g). Mix on low speed for 1–2 minutes until flour is hydrated and a rough dough forms. Cover and rest for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Oil Incorporation: Drizzle in olive oil (19.0g) and mix on medium speed for 1–2 minutes until fully absorbed into the dough matrix.
  4. Salt, Sugar & Final Knead: Add salt (24.0g), sugar (15.0g), and reserved water (29.5g). Mix for 2 minutes on medium speed until incorporated, then continue mixing for a further 1–2 minutes once the dough pulls back together into a smooth ball.
  5. Bulk Ferment: Transfer dough to a counter or tub. Cover and leave to rise at room temperature for ~1 hour (less if hot, more if cold).
  6. Balling & Cold Ferment: Divide dough into 6 x 270g portions, shape into tight round balls, and place in a proofing box. Refrigerate for approximately 72 hours.

Baking & Oven Instructions

  1. Dough Tempering: Remove dough balls from the refrigerator 1–2 hours prior to baking (depending on ambient room temperature) to allow them to warm up and stretch easily.
  2. Preheat Oven: Launch your Gozney oven and preheat for approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour until it reaches around 400°C–420°C air temperature and 380°C–390°C stone floor temperature.
  3. The Initial Launch & Door Rest: Stretch, top, and launch the pizza onto the stone floor, then immediately turn off the flame. Place the door on the oven and leave for 1 minute (rotating the pizza 180° halfway through if possible).
  4. Bake & Rotate: Remove the oven door and turn the flame back on to medium/medium-low. Bake pizza, rotating as necessary for an even char.
  5. Post-Bake & Serve: Remove once done and place directly onto a wire cooling rack for 1 minute to keep the bottom crisp. Add post-bake toppings and serve.
