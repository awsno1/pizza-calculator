Neapolitan Dough Calculator

Recipe Inputs

Ingredients Needed

Total Dough Mass: 1000 g

  • Flour 594.5 g
  • Water 386.4 g
  • Salt 17.8 g
  • Yeast 1.2 g

Method & Instructions

  1. Dissolve yeast and salt into cold water.
  2. Gradually add flour while mixing until a rough dough forms.
  3. Knead until smooth, then cover and rest at room temperature.
  4. Ball the dough into 4 x 250g portions and proof before baking.
